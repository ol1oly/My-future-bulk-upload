myFuture bulk upload

A bookmark that uploads your CVs, transcript and cover letters, builds an application package for each CV, and removes the old dated copies. It runs on your myFuture documents page.

myFuture bulk

Drag this to your bookmarks bar. It does nothing until you click it on the documents page.

This page installs version __VERSION__. Already have the bookmark? Its panel shows the version next to the title. If that number is lower, delete the old bookmark and drag this one up again.

  1. Add the bookmark. Drag the red button up to your bookmarks bar (show it with Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Open the documents page. Sign in to myFuture and go to your documents (Jobs, Internships & Volunteering › Documents).
  3. Click the bookmark. A panel opens in the corner with your current documents already loaded. Drop in your PDFs, press Review changes to see exactly what will be uploaded, built and removed, optionally Check with myFuture (a test run that saves nothing), then Apply.

Smart search for job postings

A second bookmark that searches every internship or job posting on myFuture at once: full text in English and French, filters myFuture doesn't have (apply through McGill, work language, student year, no cover letter, deadline), a match score from your keywords, and Shortlist / Apply buttons. Click it on Job Postings › View all available postings. Same as the myFuture Toolkit extension's Smart search.

myFuture search

Drag this to your bookmarks bar too. It reads postings only when you click it on a postings page.

What it does

  • Uploads the CVs, transcript and cover letters you drop in, dating each name. A name already on myFuture gets _2 instead of clashing.
  • Builds one application package per CV. Each cover letter can join a CV’s package, get its own package, or just upload. CVs and a transcript already on myFuture can be reused.
  • Removes older dated copies once everything else worked, or only cleans up with Clean up only. Names without a date and anything attached to a job you applied to are never removed.
  • Remembers your choices for next time, works in English or French, and can be dragged or minimised out of the way.
No bookmarks bar? Paste it into the console instead

On the documents page, press F12, open Console, paste this and press Enter.
